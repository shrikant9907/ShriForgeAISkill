# Profile: Healthcare & Clinic

Use for hospitals, dental clinics, specialty medical practices, and therapy clinics.

## Typical Audiences

- prospective patients seeking care for specific symptoms or conditions;
- existing patients scheduling follow-ups or checking clinic hours;
- caregivers and family members coordinating appointments and directions;
- emergency seekers looking for immediate assistance.

## Common Intents

- verify clinic location, parking, accessibility, and operating hours;
- understand medical services, treatments, and procedures offered;
- check practitioner credentials, qualifications, and specializations;
- book appointments or request consultations easily on mobile devices;
- review insurance network acceptance and payment policies;
- find clear emergency contact advice or after-hours triage guidance.

## Useful Surfaces

Home, About/Leadership, Doctors/Specialists, Services/Treatments, Patient Resources, Insurance & Billing, Appointment Booking/Contact, Emergency/Urgent Care Notice.

## Claim & Trust Policy

Strict compliance per Project Truth:
- Never fabricate doctor credentials, degrees, board certifications, or licenses;
- Never make unverified medical efficacy guarantees (e.g. "100% cure rate", "painless guaranteed");
- Include clear emergency disclaimers ("If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call emergency services immediately");
- Protect patient privacy: appointment booking forms must only collect necessary information and must not expose health data insecurely.

## Schema & SEO

Implement Schema.org `MedicalClinic` or `Physician` with verified `address`, `telephone`, `openingHours`, and `geo` coordinates.
