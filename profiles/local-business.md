# Profile: Local Business

Prioritize:
- what the business does;
- who/where it serves;
- verified location/service area;
- opening/contact details;
- services and pricing only when supplied;
- trust evidence that can be substantiated;
- call/book/request-quote/directions actions.

Local pages must contain genuinely distinct information. Never generate fake locations, reviews, ratings, certifications, or years in business.
