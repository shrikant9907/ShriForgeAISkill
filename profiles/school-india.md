# Profile: School / College in India

Use for Indian school/college public websites.

## Typical audiences

- prospective/current parents;
- students;
- staff/applicants;
- local community.

## Common intents

- understand school identity and educational offering;
- evaluate trust, leadership, safety, culture, facilities;
- understand classes/curriculum where verified;
- check admissions/process/forms;
- find fees when publication is authorized;
- find transport/location/contact information;
- read notices/events/news/results where applicable;
- access mandatory/regulatory disclosures when applicable to the institution.

## Useful surfaces

Home, About, Academics, Admissions, Facilities, Student Life, Notices/News/Events, Gallery, Contact/Location, Careers, and Regulatory/Mandatory Information when actually required. Do not create every page automatically.

## Claim policy

Verified-only:
- board affiliation and affiliation number;
- establishment year;
- awards/rankings;
- accreditations;
- pass percentages/results statistics;
- student/teacher counts;
- fees;
- address/phone/email;
- leadership names;
- testimonials;
- regulatory claims.

## UX/search notes

Mobile access is especially important for phone, WhatsApp (only if supplied/appropriate), directions, admissions, notices, and contact actions. Local-search data must exactly match verified project truth.
