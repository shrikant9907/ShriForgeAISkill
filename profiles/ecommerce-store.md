# Profile: E-Commerce Storefront

Use for retail, direct-to-consumer (D2C), or multi-product online storefronts.

## Typical Audiences

- prospective shoppers comparing options;
- return customers looking for reorders or order status;
- customer support and returns seekers.

## Common Intents

- locate products through search, categories, and filters;
- evaluate product details: specifications, materials, sizing, compatibility;
- check availability, real pricing, shipping costs, and delivery estimates;
- review genuine customer feedback without deception;
- complete checkout with minimal friction and secure payment options;
- understand returns, refund policies, and warranty information.

## Useful Surfaces

Home, Collection/Category Pages, Product Detail Pages (PDP), Cart, Checkout, Order Confirmation, FAQ/Shipping/Returns, Contact/Support.

## Claim & Trust Policy

Verified-only:
- product pricing, discounts, and promotional expiration dates;
- inventory status (do not show fake "only 1 left in stock" urgency cues unless real);
- customer reviews and ratings (never fabricate star counts or customer quotes);
- manufacturer warranties, certifications, and ingredient/material claims;
- return window duration (e.g. "30-day hassle-free returns" must match actual policy).

## Schema & SEO

Implement Schema.org `Product`, `Offer`, and `BreadcrumbList`. Ensure mobile tap targets on variant pickers and add-to-cart buttons meet accessibility guidelines.
