# Content

Write for the audience's actual questions.

Content should be:
- factually supported;
- specific where evidence exists;
- concise but complete;
- structured with useful headings;
- consistent in terminology;
- free of unsupported superlatives;
- action-oriented where appropriate.

Avoid repetitive AI clichés, manufactured urgency, fake social proof, and keyword-stuffed paragraphs.

When facts are missing, do not fill the gap with plausible-sounding claims.
