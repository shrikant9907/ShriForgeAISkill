# Product Requirements

Define what the site must accomplish before building large surfaces.

Capture:
- audiences and top tasks;
- business outcome and primary CTA;
- content/data sources;
- required integrations;
- compliance/market constraints supplied by the project;
- explicit non-goals;
- success/acceptance conditions.

Separate requirements from assumptions. If a missing detail is non-blocking, choose a reversible default rather than stopping unnecessarily.
