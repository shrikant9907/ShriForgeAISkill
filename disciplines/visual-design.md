# Visual Design

Create a deliberate identity appropriate to the organization and audience.

Establish:
- typography roles and hierarchy;
- spacing/rhythm;
- color tokens with accessible contrast;
- image/illustration direction;
- card/surface/border treatment;
- icon style;
- motion principles;
- component states.

Avoid generic AI aesthetics: indiscriminate gradients, oversized empty hero areas, random glassmorphism, excessive pill UI, decorative dashboards on non-SaaS sites, and meaningless motion.

Use visual emphasis to clarify content and actions, not merely to decorate.
