# SEO

Technical/search fundamentals should make useful pages crawlable, understandable, and internally connected.

Review where applicable:
- unique titles and descriptions;
- canonical URLs;
- indexing directives;
- sitemap and robots behavior;
- semantic heading structure;
- descriptive URLs;
- crawlable primary content;
- internal linking and orphan pages;
- image alt text when images convey content;
- social preview metadata where useful;
- redirects during URL changes.

Do not treat meta keywords, repeated target phrases, or large quantities of thin pages as a substitute for useful content.
