# UX

Design for comprehension, confidence, and task completion.

Check:
- immediate identity/purpose;
- hierarchy and scanability;
- visible next actions;
- understandable navigation;
- trust evidence near high-consideration actions;
- forms with clear labels, validation, errors, success states;
- no interaction traps or hidden essential information;
- sensible empty/loading/error states;
- mobile primary actions remain reachable.

Prefer clarity over novelty.
