# AEO / GEO / Answer Readability

Make important information easy for users and modern search/answer systems to interpret.

Use:
- concise factual summaries;
- clear entity names and relationships;
- question/answer sections only when they answer real questions;
- descriptive headings;
- structured tables/lists when they improve comprehension;
- authoritative internal links;
- consistent facts across pages.

Do not generate fake FAQs, synthetic citations, or unsupported claims merely to influence AI/search systems.
